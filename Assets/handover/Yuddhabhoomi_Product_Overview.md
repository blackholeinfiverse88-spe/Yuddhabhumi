# Yuddhabhoomi Product Overview

## Core Identity
* **What Yuddhabhoomi is:** A tactical, VR strategy game utilizing mechanics similar to Clash Royale, set in a medieval universe featuring dynamic unit deployments like knights and archers.
* **Why it exists:** To bring fast-paced, resource-managed strategy combat into immersive 3D space, forcing players to think spatially and tactically in real-time.
* **Current product vision:** A polished vertical slice featuring a fully functional VR tutorial, core navigation, HUD interactions, and elixir-based deployment loops.
* **Long-term vision:** A fully scalable multiplayer ecosystem with robust replayability, complex AI behavior traces, and deep tactical variety.

## Philosophy
* **Core philosophy:** Every deployment matters. Resource management (Elixir) and spatial awareness in VR dictate the flow of battle.
* **Strategic philosophy:** Anticipation beats reaction. Players must read the board state and manage their elixir economy to counter-push effectively.
* **Difference from traditional games:** Moving from a flat 2D screen to a 3D Unity environment requires physical situational awareness; the HUD and board state are read through natural head tracking and VR controller mapping rather than screen UI.

## The Karma System
* **Karma is:** An underlying behavioral explanation system. It dictates and tracks the cause-and-effect of unit interactions, trace histories, and strategic decisions, providing a narrative and systemic reason for *why* a battle unfolds a certain way. 
* **Karma is NOT:** A power buff, a stat multiplier, or a temporary boost to attack or defense. It does not exist to make units artificially stronger.

## Immutable Rules
* **What must never be violated:** 1. The Karma system must remain an explanatory mechanic, never a raw stat buff.
  2. Elixir management must remain the sole gatekeeper for unit deployment.
  3. VR interactions must prioritize comfort and clarity; the HUD must never obscure the primary battle arena.
