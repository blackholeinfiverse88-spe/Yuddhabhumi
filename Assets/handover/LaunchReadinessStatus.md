# Launch Readiness Status

## "If Launch Was Today" Assessment
* **What works?:** The VR tutorial is fully playable from start to finish.
* **What fails?:** Heavy combat scenarios crash the Quest due to garbage collection spikes.
* **What crashes?:** Spawning more than 30 medieval units simultaneously.
* **What confuses players?:** The distinction between standard behavioral traces and critical Karma events in the post-match screen.
* **What blocks Meta submission?:** Lack of proper VRC (Virtual Reality Checks) compliance for framerate stability.
* **What can ship?:** The tutorial and 1v1 localized prototype.
* **What must be delayed?:** The Replay system and complex AI scaling.

## Launch Estimates
* **Minimum Playable Launch estimate:** 4 weeks (requires object pooling implementation).
* **Strong Public Launch estimate:** 12 weeks.
* **Production Launch estimate:** Q4.
